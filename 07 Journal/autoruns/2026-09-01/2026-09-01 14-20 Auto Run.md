## Findings

**1. All 13 quoted `#include`s in the tree came from #62 and #63** (`#prio/medium`).
`CONVENTIONS.md` § *Includes* is explicit: angle brackets throughout, tests included, "never
`"FormatBuffer.hpp"`", and 372 of the 385 `#include` lines under `engine/`, `apps/` and `sdk/`
follow it. The 13 that do not are `engine/app/src/App.cpp:3-5` plus the mirrored set under
`apps/editor/` and `apps/runtime/`. **Nothing mechanical can catch this**: `.clang-format`
regroups includes but never rewrites the delimiter, and `misc-include-cleaner` is absent from
`.clang-tidy`'s deliberately conservative set. It is not only cosmetic; the two delimiters are
different groups to `IncludeBlocks: Regroup`, so `apps/editor/src/EditorApp.hpp` splits
`<TechEngine/core/FrameContext.hpp>` (`:3`) from `"TechEngine/app/App.hpp"` (`:5`) into two
blocks, where one delimiter would sort `app` above `core` in a single group.

**2. `engine/app` declares `platform` PRIVATE while its public header uses it** (`#prio/low`).
`engine/app/CMakeLists.txt:6` says `DEPS_PRIVATE platform`, and since #63 `App.hpp` includes
`FileAccess.hpp` and `MountTable.hpp` (`:8-9`) and holds both as members (`:16-17`). ADR-008 §8
and `CONVENTIONS.md` § *CMake* both say PUBLIC in that case. It compiles because `core` links
`platform` PUBLIC (`engine/core/CMakeLists.txt:21`) and `app` takes `core` PUBLIC, so the
include directories arrive by a different edge. **Every executable links `core`, so this can
essentially never go red**, which is exactly why it needs writing down rather than a gate.

**3. ADR-017 § *Decision* 3's `main()` clause did not ship either** (`#prio/low`). The clause
says `main()` lives in a header included once per executable; [[Project - Design]] § *The entry
point* repeats it. What shipped is a `runApp<AppType>()` template in `EntryPoint.hpp:8-14`,
with each exe keeping its own `main()`. The shipped shape is the better one, and the ADR's
own § *Consequences* worry (`Catch2WithMain` giving `TechEngineAppTests` a second `main()`)
is moot because of it. **The point is the record, not the code:** the four-pure-virtuals
divergence in the same clause is already logged in [[Project - Design]] § *The App base class*
as owing a fix or a dated amendment, and whoever writes that amendment would miss this half.

**4. [[Project - Design]] still reads as pre-build in three places** (`#prio/low`). Its header
says "**Status:** draft, nothing built" while § *`techengine_app()`, as shipped* and § *The App
base class* both describe merged code. § *Testing an executable*'s cmake sketch shows the
`$<TARGET_OBJECTS:>` splice that the later section records as **rejected**. And its
§ *Consequences* still asks for S5-T5's `done:` clause to be reworded, which happened on
[[Sprint Board]] on Aug 31; ADR-017 § *Consequences* carries the same satisfied bullet.

**Confirmed accurate, so grooming can trust them.** ADR-017 § *Consequences*' description of
`techengine_app()` matches the helper as shipped: three targets and an append to
`TE_TEST_TARGETS` (`cmake/techengine_app.cmake:22`, `:48`, `:58`, `:73`). The rest of
§ *Decision* 3 holds: `App` owns `MountTable`, `FileAccess`, `Clock`, `JobSystem` and
`FrameLoop` (`App.hpp:16-21`), the virtuals take `const FrameContext&` (`:35`, `:37`), and no
`std::function` dispatch member exists. [[Project - Design]] § *`techengine_app()`, as shipped*
is right on both of its "two calls differ" points (`techengine_app.cmake:27` and `:48-49`).
ADR-006's DAG is untouched: `app` is still `DEPS core base`, both apps still `app client core`.
ADR-015 §2's "`update()` issues no GL call" holds trivially: both overrides only log.
[[Known Issues]] **D4** already covers `toString(Role)` allocating per frame, so #63's new
`FrameContext.hpp` helper is logged, not missed.
