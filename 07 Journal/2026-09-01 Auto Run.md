### The card's second question: `/weekly-review` or CI?

**Split it, because the two failure modes are not the same check.**

| | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Exists + in range** | **CI**, and it is cheap | It catches exactly the dangling class (finding 3) and nothing else. Both runs together found ~14 wrong sites and this would have caught **3**. |
| **Points at the right thing** | **`/weekly-review`** | Every other wrong site was arithmetically valid and semantically wrong. No mechanical check reaches that. |
| **Snapshot citations** | **Neither — fix the convention** | Anchored to a sha or tag, they stop rotting and both checks skip them correctly. Today they would produce permanent false positives. |

**One caveat on the CI half.** The vault is a separate repo with its own HEAD, so a vault-side
job has to check the engine out at *some* sha, and the only defensible one is the Dashboard
stamp — which a ceremony advances. So the CI check is really "no citation dangles as of the last
reconciliation", not "as of `master`". That is still worth having; it just is not live.
