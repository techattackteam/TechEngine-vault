# 📺 Channel

How TechEngine is shown to developers on YouTube. It is the engine company's first step
([[Vision]] § *What the company is*): developers who follow the channel are its first users.
Decided 2026-10-23, Miguel as product owner.

## Three kinds of video

| Kind | What it shows | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Showcase** | One engine system, as it lands (the renderer, voxel terrain, networking), in a scene shaped like the [[Reference Game]] | **At least one a month at the start.** Each engine goal on the [[Roadmap]] ends in one. |
| **Devlog** | A TechAttack stage, showing the game grow and the engine carrying it | One per stage, in Phase 2 |
| **Short** | Something fun or surprising a system just did | Any time; informal and outside the plan |

Tutorials (how to make a game with TechEngine) wait until the SDK and the editor are stable
enough to teach.

## How a video fits the plan

- **A showcase is part of its goal's Definition of Done.** The goal is not done until its video
  is out.
- **Recording and editing are real work**, so each showcase gets its own card in the goal's
  last sprint, sized like any other card and counted against capacity.
- **Goals are sized to about a month** so the cadence holds. A goal that grows past that is
  split, and its first half gets the showcase.
- **Shorts never get a card.** If a short would cost a planned slot, it waits.
